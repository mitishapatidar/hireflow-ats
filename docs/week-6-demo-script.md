# HireFlow ATS & CRM — Week 6 Executive Demo Script (Phase 2)

## Target Audience: Agency Founders, Managing Directors & Head of Recruitment Operations
**Tenant:** Aureva Capital Private Limited

---

## 1. Demo Flow & Milestones

### Scene 1: Recruiter Mobile Experience on iPhone (Frame 46 & Frame 47)
1. **Open Mobile Web App**:
   - Navigate to `/mobile/today` on mobile viewport (390x844).
   - Show Greeting: "Hello, Recruiter 👋", IST date banner, and 3 urgent action chips: Interviews Today, Pending Submissions, and Awaiting Offer.
2. **Review Scheduled Interviews**:
   - Inspect today's timeline cards with Candidate, Job, Client, Time, and 1-tap "Join Meeting" Google Meet / Zoom button.
   - Tap "Log Call" or "Quick Note" floating thumb actions.
3. **Mobile Job Pipeline (Frame 47)**:
   - Navigate to `/jobs/[id]/pipeline`.
   - Demonstrate horizontal touch stage switcher (`SOURCED`, `SCREENED`, `SUBMITTED`, `INTERVIEW`, `OFFERED`, `PLACED`).
   - Advance candidate stage with 1 tap or tap "Reject".
   - Highlight thumb-friendly 44px tap targets and fixed bottom navigation bar (`Today`, `Jobs`, `Candidates`, `More`).

### Scene 2: Desktop Global Command Search (Frame 45)
1. **Trigger `Ctrl+K`**:
   - Press `Ctrl+K` on any page to open the floating command palette.
   - Type "Senior" or "Engineer".
   - Switch between entity tabs: `All`, `Candidates`, `Jobs`, and `Clients`.
   - Point out zero-leak guarantee: unsubmitted candidates and peer client commercial fees are completely masked for external roles.

### Scene 3: End-to-End Offers & Placements Lifecycle (Frame 08)
1. **Navigate to `/offers`**:
   - Show 4 live KPI cards: Released MTD, Accepted %, Pending response, Confirmed Placements + Total Placement Fee in INR.
   - Filter table by status (`ALL`, `RELEASED`, `ACCEPTED`, `JOINED`, `DRAFT`, `DECLINED`).
2. **Confirm Candidate Joining (Placement Milestone)**:
   - Select an `ACCEPTED` offer.
   - Click "Confirm Joining".
   - Enter joining date, confirmed fee, and payment terms (`NET_30`).
   - Submit: demonstrate transactional placement creation, application status update to `PLACED`, and duplicate placement prevention.

### Scene 4: Agency Analytics & Manager MIS (Frame 10 & Frame 39)
1. **Navigate to `/reports`**:
   - 4 Efficiency KPIs: Time to Hire (24 Days), Submission-to-Interview %, Interview-to-Offer %, Offer Acceptance Rate %.
   - Recruitment Conversion Funnel across 6 stages with drop-off percentages.
   - Recruiter Leaderboard ranked by confirmed placements.
2. **Navigate to `/reports/mis`**:
   - Highlight IST boundary calculations.
   - 6 team rollup metrics (Sourced, Submissions, Interviews, Offers, Placements, Total Revenue).
   - Recruiter actuals vs monthly targets progress bars.
