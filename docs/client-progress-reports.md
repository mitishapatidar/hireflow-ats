# Client Progress & Pipeline Velocity Reports (Prompt 22)

## Overview
Progress Reports (`/portal/reports`) provide client contacts with transparent visibility into recruitment turnaround velocity without exposing internal agency recruiter margins or confidential notes.

### Funnel Metrics
- **Open Positions**: Active mandates assigned to the client.
- **Profiles Submitted**: Total submissions delivered to the client across all open jobs.
- **Pending Review**: Submissions waiting for a shortlist or reject decision from the client.
- **Shortlisted**: Candidate profiles approved by the client for technical/manager rounds.
- **Interviews Scheduled & Completed**: Volume of interview rounds conducted.
- **Positions Filled**: Successfully placed candidates for the client.

### Job-by-Job Breakdown Table
Each row presents an authorized position:
- Position Title and Job Code
- Status (`ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`)
- Candidates Submitted
- Pending Client Review
- Shortlisted Count
- Interviews Conducted
- Total Placements
